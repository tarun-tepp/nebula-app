Nebula: Privacy

Nebula is a personal AI chat app used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Gmail

Nebula has read-only access to Gmail. It cannot send, change or delete email. It reads email only when the owner asks something in chat that needs it.

Where email text goes

To answer, the relevant email text is sent to the AI model the owner picked, such as OpenAI or Anthropic. The answer is saved in Nebula's private chat history, which only the owner can see or delete. Nebula never sells or shares email content and never uses it for advertising.

Sign-in

Nebula keeps the Google sign-in on its own private server so Gmail stays connected. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google data

Nebula's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
